Privacy & Terms

Harambe Escape by kungFuDOOM · Last updated October 19, 2026

What this game is

A free browser beat-'em-up you can play on the web and on itch.io. You do not need an account.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, Vercel and itch.io. Those hosts see your IP address and basic browser info when you load the page, under their own policies.

Global leaderboard

If you submit a score, we store it in Supabase:

  • the name you choose
  • your score, stage reached, and whether you won
  • a hashed random ID for this browser, used only to limit spam

Leaderboard names and scores are public. Anyone can see them.

Optional wallet

You can optionally save a Solana wallet address with your leaderboard name. We store it with the name, the hashed browser ID, and a last-seen time. Clear it by saving an empty wallet.

On your device

The game uses localStorage for saves, settings, your name and the browser ID. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Donations on itch.io

Optional donations on itch.io are handled by itch.io and PayPal under their own policies. We do not see your payment details.

Remove your score

Email kungfudoom@mail.grokbot.com with the leaderboard name you want removed.
